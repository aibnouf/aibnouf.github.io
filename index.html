Common Misconceptions About Anaesthesia

"Anaesthesia is just sleep."

It is a controlled, medically induced state affecting consciousness, pain, memory, and reflexes.

"The surgeon handles everything."

Anaesthesiologists are the physicians responsible for keeping the patient stable throughout the procedure.

"Anaesthesia is dangerous."

Modern anaesthesia is extremely safe when administered by trained specialists.

"You don't need to tell the Anaesthesiologist everything."

Full honesty about medications, smoking, or health conditions is essential for safety.
